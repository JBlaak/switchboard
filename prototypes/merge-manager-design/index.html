The Merge manager

A third manager joins the floor. You press Land it on a reviewed pull request, and from then on the Merge manager owns it: it waits its turn in its repo's lane, gets updated and tested only as the head, gets fixed by an errand when it fails out, and merges. This board shows the figure, its page, every place merging shows up, and the open questions for you at the bottom.

Already decided on the map

What the design has to carry

Two lanes, one model

Queue repos keep the merge-queue Action as the merger; others get a Switchboard lane. Both show the same stages: Waiting · Held · Head · Fixing · Needs you · Merged.

Land it

Per pull request, never on drafts, only your own. In a queue repo it adds automerge:opt-in. Unland takes it back.

Fix errands

Only when the head fails out on a conflict or a failed check. Review feedback goes to Needs you. Fixing rows show their errand; Needs you rows offer Open and Try again.

Merging host

The Mac, or one VM you hand merging off to. While a VM merges, the page shows its state with a badge and keeps a "While merging was on…" summary.

Fewest CI runs

Waiting branches are never updated; unfinished CI behind the head is cancelled; conflicts are found by a local test rebase. The page shows what that saved.

The session

Archived on merge, with a rocket instead of confetti. A busy session archives at the end of its turn; one with uncommitted changes stays and shows Merged.

The figure

A Patch that brings pull requests in to land

Two costumes to compare. The marshaller fits the word "land": ear defenders, a hi-vis vest and two lit wands. The signaller fits "lane": a peaked cap and a flag. Each has four poses. The counter control compares two meanings for the badge.

Archive

A rocket instead of confetti

Archiving any session throws confetti from the row's centre. Archiving a landed session, by hand or on merge, launches a small rocket from the same spot. Try both.

Tidy the plan tab · PR #428 merged by hand
Land it from the session header · Merged
On merge the Merge manager archives the session itself. Idle: right away, with the rocket. Busy: at the end of its turn.
Uncommitted changes keep the session open; its row shows merged until you archive it.
Reduced motion skips both, as confetti does today.
